About Us

NO FRILLS. NO EXCUSES.

REDS GYM is Oldham's latest high-quality independent gym, located in Pennine House on Denton Lane, Chadderton. Equipped with premium Nautilus and Watson equipment, we're built for bodybuilders, powerlifters, and boxing enthusiasts who want a proper gym — not a crowded corporate chain.

Owned and run by Jack, REDS is a community where everyone knows your name. No queuing for machines, no intimidating atmosphere — just quality equipment, friendly faces, and real results. Even Jack's dog Poncho is there to welcome you in.

Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Do I need experience to join? ▼

Not at all! REDS GYM welcomes everyone from complete beginners to experienced lifters. Many members were nervous before joining and now love it. Jack and the team will make you feel right at home.

Is it only for bodybuilders? ▼

No — we have bodybuilders, powerlifters, boxers, people doing general fitness, and those recovering from injuries. The equipment suits every type of training. One member said: "I thought it would be full of arrogant muscle men. It's absolutely not."

Is there a contract? ▼

No long-term contract. It's a rolling £30/month membership — cancel anytime. No joining fee. Use code BIGRED and your first month is just £5.

Can I try before I join? ▼

Yes! We offer a free day pass — full gym access for the day with no commitment. Come in, see the equipment, meet the team, and decide if it's right for you.

Is it women-friendly? ▼

Absolutely. One of our members, Nahidah, said: "As a hijabi modest girl I feel extremely welcome and comfortable." The atmosphere is friendly and inclusive — no intimidation, no judgment.

Do you offer personal training? ▼

Yes — multiple PTs operate out of REDS, including Mitchell (@1mitchellfitness). They specialise in weight loss, muscle gain, and injury rehabilitation. No contracted hours, just flexible 1-on-1 coaching.

Is there parking? ▼

The gym has great links to the motorway and is easy to find. Located at Pennine House on Denton Lane, Chadderton — easy access from Oldham, Manchester, and surrounding areas.

Do you offer boxing training? ▼

Yes! We have a dedicated boxing area with heavy bags. Boxing is one of our core offerings alongside bodybuilding and powerlifting — build power, speed, and conditioning.
